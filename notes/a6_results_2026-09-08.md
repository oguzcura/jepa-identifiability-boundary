## Process lesson (for paper limitations / methods)
Freezing an *instrument* (readout metric, control construction) inside a
decision rule without validating it can detect the effect under the target
geometry is what produced two of the three nulls. Pre-registration discipline
caught the over-claims (the bars stopped us reporting metric artifacts as
results) — but the audit, not the bars, found the instrument flaws. Future
amendments: power analysis + instrument sensitivity check BEFORE freezing bars.
